# Questions for Your Key Contact

Record each answer here, along with the date. Only recorded answers go into `radius-analysis.md`.

## What you're walking into
1. What are the top one or two outcomes you need from me in the first 30 days?
2. What's going well right now, and what's causing the most trouble?
3. Who else should I talk to first?
4. Is there anything I should avoid assuming or doing early on?

## Access and blockers
5. What can I usefully do before my laptop arrives and my pre-start checks clear?
6. Who owns laptop and access setup, and when should I expect them?
7. Which documents could I see now, and which have to wait?

## Radius Analysis
8. Which system or project should I look at first?
9. Who owns the platform and network layers that system relies on?
10. Which enterprise controls will shape the work, such as security, compliance, or change approval?
11. Which outside vendors or providers does it depend on?

## Working together
12. How and how often would you like to meet and get updates?
