# Intent: Onboarding

## Intent
Prepare a new starter for onboarding this week: get connected with their key contact, understand the role they're walking into, and start Radius Analysis without waiting for perfect access.

## Inputs / Context
- Source: `examples/onboarding-notes.md`
- No company laptop yet
- Pre-start checks underway
- Key contact's details missing

## Outputs
- Draft messages to whoever can provide the key contact's details, and to the key contact
- This workspace, including a Day 1–5 plan
- A list of blockers, split into what can be done now and what needs the new starter or outside access

## Success Criteria
- The new starter is in contact with their key contact.
- The new starter can state what the role involves, based on answers rather than assumptions.
- Radius Analysis has started and shows what's known and unknown.
- Nothing is sent without the new starter's explicit approval.

## Constraints
- Create only the minimum useful artifacts, then advance based on evidence.
- Don't state anything about the company, the key contact, or the role unless it comes from the notes or from a recorded answer.
